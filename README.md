## 📌 Overview

This document presents a complete **Software Requirements Analysis** for an AI-powered Chatbot system — covering users, stakeholders, functional & non-functional requirements, risks, quality attributes, and the role of AI.

---

## 👥 A. Users

| # | User Type |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | 🎓 Students |
| 2 | 💼 Employees |
| 3 | 🎧 Customer Service Representatives |
| 4 | 🌐 General Users |

---

## 🎯 B. Purpose

The chatbot communicates with users through **natural language processing**. It is designed to:

- 💬 **Answer questions** from users instantly
- 🧭 **Guide users** through tasks and information

---

## 🏢 C. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role |
|-------------|------|
| 👤 Users | Receive information and assistance from the chatbot |
| 🏬 Business | Uses the chatbot to improve service quality |
| 👨‍💻 Developers | Develop, maintain, monitor, and update the chatbot |

---

## ⚙️ D. Functional Requirements

- ✅ The chatbot **shall accept** user questions in natural language
- ✅ It **shall provide** relevant and accurate responses
- ✅ It **shall analyze** and maintain conversation context
- ✅ It **shall store** all chat data securely

---

## 📊 E. Non-Functional Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| ⚡ Performance | Should respond within a few seconds |
| 🔒 Privacy | User personal information must be protected |
| 🖥️ Usability | Interface should be simple and user-friendly |

---

## ⚠️ F. Risks

> These are potential risks that must be managed during development.

- ❌ **Inaccurate Information** — The chatbot may provide wrong answers
- 🔓 **Data Exposure** — User information could be leaked or exposed

---

## 🏆 G. Quality Attributes

```
✔ Reliability  →  Consistently performs correctly
✔ Usability    →  Easy to use for all types of users
✔ Security     →  Protects user data from unauthorized access
```

---

## 🧠 H. Where AI Could Be Used

AI plays a key role in the chatbot by enabling it to:

- 🗣️ **Understand natural language** (NLP)
- 🤖 **Generate human-like responses**
- 📝 **Summarize long conversations**

---

## 🚨 I. What Could Go Wrong if AI Is Used Incorrectly

| Risk | Impact |
|------|--------|
| 📰 Misinformation | AI may generate false or misleading answers |
| ⚖️ Bias | Responses may reflect unfair training data biases |
| 🔏 Privacy Violations | Sensitive user data may be mishandled |
